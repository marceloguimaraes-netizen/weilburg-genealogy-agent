# Sources beyond FamilySearch — Weilburg / Nassau and emigrant destinations

Before relying on any entry, check what is online now: collections move and
coverage changes. Entries marked *(verify)* are leads to confirm, not established facts.

## Historical context (which records exist)

| Period | Jurisdiction | Main vital records |
|---|---|---|
| before 1806 | County/Principality of Nassau-Weilburg | Church books only (Lutheran in Weilburg; Catholic/Reformed parishes nearby) |
| 1806–1866 | Duchy of Nassau (capital Wiesbaden) | Church books; from 1818, pastors also kept **duplicate registers** sent to the state *(verify coverage per parish)* |
| 1866–1874 | Kingdom of Prussia, prov. Hesse-Nassau, Regierungsbezirk Wiesbaden | Church books |
| from 1 Oct 1874 | Prussia (Hesse from 1945) | **Civil registration (Standesamt)** — births, marriages, deaths. Registers become public archive records after 110 y (births), 80 y (marriages), 30 y (deaths) under the Personenstandsgesetz |

Weilburg was the residence of the Nassau-Weilburg line, so court, administrative and military
(*Nassauische Truppen*) records exist in addition to parish records.

## Germany — online

- **Archion** (archion.de, paid) — scans of Protestant church books; check the EKHN
  (Evangelische Kirche in Hessen und Nassau) holdings for Weilburg and surrounding parishes.
- **Matricula** (data.matricula-online.eu, free) — Catholic registers; check whether the
  **Bistum Limburg** parishes are online; otherwise write to the Diözesanarchiv Limburg.
- **Arcinsys Hessen** (arcinsys.hessen.de) — catalogue of the Hessian state archives,
  including the **Hessisches Hauptstaatsarchiv Wiesbaden (HHStAW)**, which holds the Duchy of Nassau
  records: church-book duplicates, civil registers, emigration files, tax lists, court and
  military files. Search by place name and surname; many items are digitised.
- **LAGIS Hessen** (lagis-hessen.de) — historical gazetteer (which parish a village belonged
  to and when), maps, and emigrant databases (*verify current names of databases*).
- **Ortsfamilienbücher** — online-ofb.de and printed OFBs (compiled family reconstitutions for a village). Derivative, but excellent for cross-checking.
- **GenWiki / CompGen** (wiki.genealogy.net) — parish pages, GEDBAS user trees, the
  *Verlustlisten 1. Weltkrieg* (WWI casualty lists) index, address books.
- **Meyers Gazetteer** (meyersgaz.org) — locating places as of 1871–1912 and their civil and church jurisdictions.
- **Deutsches Zeitungsportal** (deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/newspaper) — digitised newspapers (e.g. *Weilburger Tageblatt*, Nassau official gazettes, *verify titles*): death notices, emigration announcements, estate sales.
- **Volksbund Gräbersuche** — war dead from WWI/WWII.

## Germany — offline / on request

- **Stadtarchiv Weilburg** and **Kreisarchiv Limburg-Weilburg** — local records, Standesamt duplicates, *Bürgerbücher*, property and tax records.
- **Standesamt Weilburg** — civil records still within the privacy periods (proof of descent required).
- **Zentralarchiv der EKHN** (Darmstadt) / **Diözesanarchiv Limburg** — church books not online.
- **Hessische Familiengeschichtliche Vereinigung (HFV)** — local researchers, OFB publications, query service.

## Emigration and travel

- **HHStAW** — Nassau required permission to emigrate; release files (*Auswanderungsakten*) often name destination and family members. Search Arcinsys by surname + "Auswanderung".
- **Hamburg passenger lists 1850–1934** (Hamburg State Archive; indexed on Ancestry).
- **Bremen passenger lists** (passagierlisten.de) — most pre-1920 lists were destroyed; surviving ones 1920–1939.
- **Antwerp / Le Havre** — many Nassauers left through these ports; arrival records in the destination country are usually the better source.

## Brazil (if relevant)

- **Arquivo Nacional** (SIAN) — arrival lists for Rio de Janeiro, naturalisation.
- **Museu da Imigração do Estado de São Paulo** — Hospedaria de Imigrantes registers and ship lists (digital collection).
- **Arquivo Histórico de Petrópolis / Joinville / São Leopoldo** — German colonies with many Hesse/Nassau settlers.
- **Hemeroteca Digital Brasileira** (Biblioteca Nacional) — newspapers, including German-language Brazilian papers.
- **Cartórios de Registro Civil** — civil registration from 1889 (obligatory from 1890 onward); before that, parish registers (Catholic dioceses, Lutheran IECLB congregations).

## USA (if relevant)

- **Castle Garden / Ellis Island** arrival records (pre-1892 / 1892–1924), NARA naturalisation files, US census 1850–1950, state vital records, German-language church records (Lutheran/Catholic), Find a Grave / BillionGraves.
- Texas: the *Adelsverein* settlements (New Braunfels, Fredericksburg) drew many Nassau emigrants in the 1840s.

## Independent cross-checks

- **DNA** — Ancestry, MyHeritage, FTDNA, GEDmatch: shared-match clusters confirm or refute a link three to six generations back; Y-DNA tests the paternal line.
- **Other trees** (MyHeritage, Geni, Ancestry, GEDBAS) — only as hints; they often copy the same mistake.
